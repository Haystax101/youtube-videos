# Fact catalogue (prototype, 2026-11-25)

Twenty "wait, what?" facts in the spirit of QI / *No Such Thing As A Fish*, each researched
independently and written in our own words, with a Gary premise and a likely ending. Nothing here
is copied from the podcast: their public feed only gives episode titles and a few topic words, and
we use those as a reading list, never as a source. Every number still gets checked against the
source again before it goes in a script's `experiment.facts`.

Status: **checked** = confirmed against the listed sources on 2026-11-25. **check** = from memory
or a single secondary source; verify before scripting.

"Fish lead" = a Fish episode whose title or topic words touch the same subject (keyword match in
their public RSS feed, not proof they told this exact fact). Credit line idea for descriptions:
"Fact rabbit-hole inspired by No Such Thing As A Fish. Sources below."

## How to grow this

- **Lead list:** `/mnt/project-files/gary-tries-it/research/fish-episode-topics.csv` (private project
  folder, not this public repo) lists all 746 feed episodes (2014-2026) with their topic words.
  A topic like "very specific skunk" or "radiopharmaceuticals" is a prompt to go and research,
  not a fact.
- **george's own listening notes** are the best lead: a one-line "they said X about Y" note, and
  the script writer finds the original study and writes it fresh.
- Never transcribe or quote episodes, never use their audio, logo or show name in a title; a
  credit line in the description is fine and friendly.
