Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

The Gary Tries It production tool is used only by the owner of the Gary Tries It YouTube channel, on their own Google account. It is not offered to the public and does not let anyone else sign in.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, through Google OAuth, the tool accesses the owner's YouTube channel to: upload videos and set their titles and descriptions; read video and channel statistics and YouTube Analytics reports (such as views, watch time and audience retention); and read public comments on the channel's videos.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish the channel's videos and decide what to make next. Aggregated statistics and public comment text may be stored in the channel's project repository. No personal data about viewers is collected, sold or shared with third parties, and no data is used for advertising.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
